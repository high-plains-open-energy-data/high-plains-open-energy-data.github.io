Wyoming's transmission network

Map data as of 20 October 2026.

A map of the power lines and substations in Wyoming at 69 kV and above, built entirely from public data. The lines and substations come from OpenStreetMap, which volunteers map and which is detailed in Wyoming, though neither complete nor official.

The map shows 1,799 line sections and 578 substations. Lengths are measured inside Wyoming only; lines that cross the state line are cut at the border. About 2,392 km of lines carry no voltage in OpenStreetMap and are drawn as dashed grey lines.

Downloads

  • wyoming_transmission_lines_osm.geojson: every line section, with its OpenStreetMap ID, name, operator, voltage tag, voltage class, the counties it crosses and its length in Wyoming.
  • wyoming_substations_osm.geojson: every substation, with its OpenStreetMap ID, name, operator, voltage tag and county.
  • The map above, as an image.

GitHub displays GeoJSON files as an interactive map: open either file in the repository to zoom in and click on any line or substation.
