Wyoming generation tracker

As of 14 November 2026. Statuses are as reported to EIA for 31 December 2025 in the EIA-860 2025 data release, the most recent final data. They will be updated when EIA publishes newer data.

Summary

For 2025, developers and utilities reported 17 active proposed generators in Wyoming, totalling 7,206.6 MW, against 12,735.6 MW of existing capacity. Of that, 3,550.5 MW was under construction.

  • Onshore Wind Turbine: 4,060.6 MW
  • Solar Photovoltaic: 1,427.0 MW
  • Batteries: 1,099.0 MW
  • Nuclear: 500.0 MW
  • Natural Gas Fired Combustion Turbine: 120.0 MW

A further 5 generators (621.1 MW) were reported as indefinitely postponed or cancelled, and are listed separately below. Two existing generators report a planned retirement.

Reading this tracker

  • Every entry comes from EIA Form 860, as processed by PUDL and checked against EIA's own release. The EIA plant ID links each row to EIA's records.
  • Proposed projects and dates are reported by their developers. Many proposed projects are delayed or never built, and planned dates often move.
  • Status codes show each generator's status on 31 December 2025, using EIA's definitions: P, planned with regulatory approvals not initiated; L, approvals pending; T, approvals received; U and V, under construction (up to and above half complete); TS, built but not yet in commercial operation; IP, indefinitely postponed; CN, cancelled.
  • Battery storage paired with a solar project is listed as its own generator, as EIA reports it.
