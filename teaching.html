Teaching

Where does Wyoming's electricity come from, and where does it go?

An undergraduate lesson, about 90 minutes, built on the same open data as this site. Students load three PUDL tables, compare capacity with generation, find the coal units converted to natural gas, estimate how much of Wyoming's electricity leaves the state, and look at what is proposed.

  • Jupyter notebook (Python, pandas, pyarrow, matplotlib)
  • Student worksheet (Word)
  • Teacher notes with answers (Word)

Level: first- or second-year undergraduates in engineering, environmental science, economics or data science. Prerequisites: basic Python and pandas.

The notebook reads PUDL's current "stable" release. The answers in the teacher notes come from the release published on 10 November 2026, so later releases may change some figures slightly.

Teachers who use the lesson are warmly invited to open an issue with feedback.

As of 15 November 2026.
