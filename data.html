Wyoming electricity generation, 2025

Dataset as of 14 November 2026. Covers generators located in Wyoming as reported to EIA for 2025, the most recent year of final data.

Downloads

  • wyoming_generators_2025.csv: all 132 existing generators, with plant, utility, county, coordinates, balancing authority, fuel, technology, status, capacity and dates.
  • wyoming_capacity_generation_by_fuel_2025.csv: the summary above.
  • wyoming_proposed_generators_2025.csv and wyoming_planned_retirements_2025.csv: the data behind the monitor.
  • checks.json: the totals and cross-checks recorded when the data was built.

Sources

  • PUDL table out_eia__yearly_generators (EIA Forms 860 and 923): generators, capacity and status.
  • PUDL table out_eia923__yearly_generation_fuel_combined (EIA Form 923): net generation by plant and fuel.
  • EIA-860 2025 data release, file 3_1_Generator_Y2025.xlsx, used to cross-check the PUDL figures.

Checks

Every build compares the PUDL figures with the EIA-860 workbook itself. For 2025:

  • Existing generators: 132 generators and 12,735.6 MW in PUDL, against 132 generators and 12,735.6 MW on EIA-860's operable schedule. They match exactly.
  • Active proposed generators: 17 generators and 7,206.6 MW in PUDL, against 17 generators and 7,206.6 MW on EIA-860's proposed schedule. They match exactly once indefinitely postponed and cancelled projects are excluded, as described on the monitor page.

Notes and limitations

  • Capacity is nameplate capacity. Summer and winter capacities, which are usually lower, are included in the generator file.
  • Generation by fuel comes from plant-level EIA-923 data. Each generator has one primary fuel code, so units that can burn more than one fuel, or that have been converted, are counted under their primary fuel.
  • "Other" includes battery storage and small units reporting other fuels.
  • Net generation is measured at the plant; it is not the same as electricity consumed in Wyoming.

Reproduce it

Run python scripts/build_wyoming_data.py from the repository. Add --eia860 path/to/3_1_Generator_Y2025.xlsx to repeat the cross-check against EIA's release.
